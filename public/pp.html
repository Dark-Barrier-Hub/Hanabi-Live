Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data Hanabi Live collects, why we collect it, and how you can control it. We believe in transparency and minimal data collection.
01

Overview

Hanabi Live ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our anime streaming platform.

We use Firebase (a Google service) to handle authentication and data storage. By using Hanabi Live, you also agree to Google's data practices as described in their Privacy Policy, which we reference in Section 5.

This policy applies to all users of Hanabi Live regardless of how you access the service — via web browser, mobile browser, or any future application.

02

Data We Collect

We collect information in three ways: information you provide directly, information collected automatically, and information from third parties (Google).

Category Data Collected Source
Account Name, email address, profile photo, Google UID Google Sign-In
Profile Custom profile photo (base64), display name preferences You (optional)
Activity Favorited anime IDs, watch history, comments posted Your usage

We do not collect: Payment information (we have no paid tiers), government IDs, precise location data, or sensitive personal information beyond what is listed above.
03

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service provision: To create and manage your account, display your profile, and enable platform features like favorites and comments.
  • Personalization: To remember your preferences, watch history, and favorites across sessions.
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and abuse of our platform.
  • Communication: To send you important service updates, policy changes, or security alerts (no marketing emails unless opted in).
  • Analytics: To understand how our platform is used so we can improve it. Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized where possible.
  • Legal compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and lawful government requests.

We do not use your data for targeted advertising, sell it to data brokers, or share it for marketing purposes.

04

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: With trusted partners who help us operate the platform (e.g., Firebase/Google for database and authentication). These partners are bound by data processing agreements.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, subpoena, or government request, or to protect the rights and safety of Hanabi Live, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred. We will notify you before this happens.
  • With your consent: In any other circumstances, only with your explicit permission.
Public content: Comments you post on Hanabi Live are visible to all users of the platform. Please do not include personal information in public comments.
05

Firebase & Google Services

Hanabi Live uses Firebase, a Google platform, for authentication, database storage, and related services. When you sign in with Google, Firebase processes your authentication data according to Google's Privacy Policy.

The Firebase services we use include:

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages your sign-in via Google OAuth. Google processes your email, name, and profile photo to verify your identity.
  • Cloud Firestore: Stores your profile data, favorites, and account metadata in Google's secure cloud infrastructure.

Google's data centers are located primarily in the United States. By using Hanabi Live, you acknowledge that your data may be transferred to and processed in the US or other countries where Google operates data centers.

You can review Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy and Firebase's terms at firebase.google.com/terms.

06

Cookies & Local Storage

Hanabi Live uses browser localStorage (not traditional cookies) to enhance your experience. Here's what we store locally in your browser:

  • hbProfile: Cached profile data (name, photo, favorites) for fast loading without repeated network requests.
  • hbFavs: Your favorited anime IDs for offline-first access.
  • hbComments: Locally cached comments for the current session.
  • hbUid: Your Firebase user ID to maintain session state.

Firebase may also set authentication cookies in your browser. These are essential for keeping you logged in and cannot be disabled without logging out.

You can clear all locally stored data at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or by signing out and using your browser's privacy tools.

No tracking pixels or third-party ad cookies. We do not use advertising networks, retargeting pixels, or cross-site tracking technologies.
07

Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously. We implement a range of technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • All data transmission between your browser and our services is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Firebase Firestore is secured with Firebase Security Rules that restrict access to authorized users only.
  • Profile photos are stored as base64 strings within Firestore, encrypted at rest by Google.
  • We do not store passwords — authentication is handled entirely by Google OAuth.
  • Access to our Firebase project is restricted to authorized administrators with multi-factor authentication.

Despite these measures, no security system is impenetrable. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly to security@hanabilive.com.

08

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services.

  • Active accounts: Data is retained indefinitely while your account remains active.
  • Deleted accounts: Upon account deletion, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.
  • Inactive accounts: Accounts with no activity for 24+ months may be flagged for deletion. We will notify you via email before any deletion.
  • Comments: Public comments may remain visible after account deletion in anonymized form.
  • Logs: System and access logs may be retained for up to 90 days for security purposes.

10

Children's Privacy

Hanabi Live is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Users between 13 and 17 years of age may use Hanabi Live with parental consent. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided us with personal information without consent should contact us immediately at privacy@hanabilive.com, and we will take steps to delete that information.

COPPA compliance: We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If we discover that a child under 13 has created an account, we will promptly delete the account and associated data.
