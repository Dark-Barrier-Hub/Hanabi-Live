🎯 Mission

We exist to connect fans with the stories they love

Anime is more than entertainment — it's a medium that explores the full depth of human experience, from the most intimate personal struggles to universe-spanning epics. We believe everyone deserves access to these stories.

Hanabi Live was founded in 2024 by a group of long-time anime fans who were frustrated with slow, ugly, or overpriced streaming platforms. We set out to build something better — something that respects both the art and the audience.

Our platform is free to use. We fund development through community support and are committed to staying ad-free, fast, and open to fans around the world.

📅 Our Journey

The Story So Far

2024 — Founding
The Spark
I frustrated with existing platforms sketched the first version of Hanabi Live on a whiteboard. The goal: build the anime streaming platform we always wanted.
2024 — Alpha
First Build
Launched a private alpha with 50 users. The feedback was overwhelming — fast, beautiful, and exactly what the community needed.
2025 — Beta Launch
Opening the Gates
Public beta launched. 500,000 users in the first month. Introduced Google sign-in, profiles, favorites, and the comment system.
2025 — Growth
1 Million Members
Crossed 1 million registered members. Expanded the library to 1,200+ titles, added schedule tracking, and launched 4K streaming support.
2026 — Today
2M+ and Growing
With over 2 million members worldwide, we're continuing to grow the library, improve performance, and build features requested by our amazing community.
